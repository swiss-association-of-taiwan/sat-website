Have an idea for an activity? Maybe a community hike, a family day at the beach, a sports outing, a cultural visit, or simply getting a few people together for something you enjoy. Let us know. The committee can help share your idea with the SAT community, connect interested people and, where possible, help you make it happen.

## Lend a Hand

You do not need to make a big commitment to contribute. Help serve raclette at an apéro, bring some homemade bread or cookies, help with setup, take a few photos for our social media, or simply offer a hand when needed. Small contributions are always welcome and help make our gatherings feel like community events.

## Join the Committee

If you would like to get involved more regularly, consider joining the SAT committee. Committee members help shape our activities, organize events and keep the association moving forward.

If you are interested, let us know and consider putting yourself forward for election at the next AGM.
