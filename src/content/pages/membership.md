SAT is built around people who share an interest in Switzerland and in bringing the Swiss community in Taiwan together.

By becoming a member, you support the activities, traditions and gatherings that SAT organizes throughout the year and help us keep an active and welcoming community for Swiss people, their families, friends and anyone with a connection to Switzerland.

Membership is also a way to become a little more involved in the community. Members receive a special member rate for SAT events and are kept informed about upcoming gatherings and activities.

{{lead}}
Most importantly, your membership helps make these activities possible.
